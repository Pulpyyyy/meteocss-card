🌤️ MeteoCSS Depth Map Studio

Drop the greyscale depth map from Depth Anything (with the sky erased to transparency), preview the card's shadow with ground compensation, then copy the YAML or export a corrected PNG. For best results add a ground mask (white = ground, e.g. a quick select-by-color in GIMP): the ground surface is then interpolated exactly instead of estimated. Everything runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Use the PNG if you prefer pre-baked compensation (then keep ground_compensation: false in the card), or just copy the YAML below.

shadow:
  depthmap: /local/my-depthmap.png
